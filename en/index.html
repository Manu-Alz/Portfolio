(01) — About

I design and build brands and digital products for companies that need results. I work with the discipline of the mat and the patience of the ocean : measurable goals, progress every week and every detail refined until it performs.

I’m Manuel Alzugaray, an independent designer and developer based in Punta del Este, Uruguay. Since 2023 I’ve been building brands, websites and apps end to end: strategy, identity, interface, code, content and launch. I also produce and edit video, after a year of continuous content creation.

The other half of my life is sport. I train judo in the −73 kg category, coach strength training, and grew up in a family of high-performance athletes. That shapes how I work: clear goals, consistency and measured adjustments. I work in Spanish and English (C2).

(05) — Athlete

Method

I work the way
I train.

Years of planning training left me with a process: measure before acting, move in short cycles and adjust with data. I apply it the same way to a brand, a website or a video.

  1. 01

    Diagnosis

    Understand the starting point: goal, audience, competition and what already exists. Without this, everything else is guessing.

    Deliverable · brief and goals
  2. 02

    Plan

    Structure, messaging and visual direction defined before designing. A clear path with stages and dates.

    Deliverable · sitemap and references
  3. 03

    Execution

    Design and development in short cycles, with browsable versions from the first week so decisions are made on something real.

    Deliverable · live prototype
  4. 04

    Follow-up

    Launch, measurement and adjustments. The client can update on their own, and the project keeps performing afterwards.

    Deliverable · launch and metrics
